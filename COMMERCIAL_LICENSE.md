# Commercial Licensing

MCP-Bastion requires a separate paid commercial license for:

- production use by companies or organizations,
- internal enterprise deployments,
- managed services or hosted offerings,
- paid consulting or customer-delivered solutions,
- bundling with paid products.

## What to include in your request

- company name and legal entity,
- intended deployment model (internal, SaaS, on-prem),
- estimated number of servers/users,
- support/SLA requirements,
- expected contract start date.

## Contact

Please open a GitHub issue titled `Commercial License Request` or contact the project maintainer listed in this repository.

Until a signed commercial agreement is in place, commercial usage is not permitted under the community license.
